Method

How entities are recognized

All entries makes every eligible Dart and Pat accessible, whether or not a named entity is recognized. Search checks the published text, parsed target, and parsed sender. The live Google Sheet is read when the page loads or Refresh data is selected; if it cannot be reached, a cached archive is shown and labeled.

The named-entity lookup was updated September 8, 2026 after review of candidates proposed by two NER models over the September 7 snapshot. It includes approved full names, merged spelling variants and source-checked occurrences. Ambiguous or unsupported short forms remain withheld. Events includes named events, holidays and observances; Misc. retains technology, products, works and selected concepts outside other types. This is not an exhaustive inventory or an independently validated accuracy study.

New Sheet rows are checked against the same lookup automatically on refresh. The discovery models do not rerun. Some reviewed short-name matches are tied to the original text of specific records; these decisions are not reused if that text changes. Source text is never rewritten. Longer competing names take priority at the same text span, so Alpha Phi Omega is not automatically Alpha Phi. Physical cards are distinguished from Card Services, and people from similarly named buildings.

Entity counts represent records containing an entity, not repeated uses of its name. The same entry can mention several entities. Recognition does not establish that the Dart or Pat is directed at that entity. Overall coverage counts each entry once.

Topics beta uses a separate, visible keyword list. It is a starting point for exploration, not a trained topic model or sentiment analysis. Keywords can have multiple meanings and topic totals overlap. Each result shows its matching keywords; entries without a topic match remain available in All entries.

Only entries labeled Dart or Pat are included in this explorer; combined entries are excluded from all counts, charts, searches, and record lists. The balance gauge describes the selected entity and year (or all years). Equal counts are an even split; otherwise, a majority up to 55% is nearly even, above 55% but below 60% is a Dart or Pat lean, at least 60% but below 75% is a fairly strong lean, and 75% or more is a strong lean. Labels use unrounded shares. These are descriptive bands, not tests of statistical significance or measures of sentiment intensity.
