Method

How entities are recognized

The page reads the shared Google Sheet when it loads and checks the published text, parsed target, and parsed sender against a versioned JMU-specific dictionary of people, organizations, academic units, buildings, places, housing communities, businesses, groups, and the mascot. The Breeze and WXJM are grouped under Organizations.

Counts represent records containing an entity, not the number of times its name repeats inside one record. Historical aliases are grouped under one stable name. This controlled method favors interpretable, campus-specific results over speculative name extraction from OCR text.

Only entries labeled Dart or Pat are included in this explorer; combined entries are excluded from all counts, charts, searches, and record lists. The balance gauge describes the selected entity and year (or all years). A 45–55% split is nearly even; a majority above 55% but below 65% is a slight lean, at least 65% but below 80% is moderate, and 80% or more is strong. These are descriptive bands, not tests of statistical significance or measures of sentiment intensity.
